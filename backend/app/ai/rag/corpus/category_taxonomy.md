# Category Taxonomy

## Overview

This is the reference used when a complaint does not obviously belong to one
category: a short, authoritative definition of each of the twelve categories
and the hand-offs that most often cause confusion. It intentionally repeats
the boundary language from each department's SOP so a single retrieval hit
is enough to disambiguate.

## ROADS

Damage to the surface of an existing, previously serviceable road: potholes,
cracks, broken dividers, damaged footpaths and kerbs. Hands off an unfilled
utility trench to CONSTRUCTION and standing rainwater on the surface to
FLOODING.

## CONSTRUCTION

Building work, illegal or unpermitted structures, and excavation left
unrepaired on public land. Hands off a surface crack on an otherwise intact
road to ROADS and construction-site waste with no open dig to SANITATION.

## ELECTRICITY

Faults in the public power distribution network: fallen lines, damaged
poles, faulty street lighting, transformer faults, and unscheduled outages.
Hands off a pole that is structurally fine but obstructing footpath work to
ROADS and private generator noise, which is outside municipal jurisdiction
entirely, to civic enforcement rather than the Electricity Board.

## WATER

The potable water supply network: burst pipes, contaminated or low-pressure
tap water, damaged stand-posts, and faulty meters. Hands off foul water from
a drain or manhole, as opposed to the tap, to SEWAGE and long-standing
water with no active leak to FLOODING.

## SANITATION

Solid waste: overflowing bins, missed collection, illegal dumping, street
sweeping, and dead animal removal. Hands off liquid waste and sewer overflow
to SEWAGE and waste at an unsecured construction site to CONSTRUCTION.

## PUBLIC_SPACES

Parks, playgrounds, public gardens, and street furniture: damaged play
equipment, overgrown vegetation, vandalised benches or monuments. Hands off
a faulty light pole inside a park to ELECTRICITY and an encroachment built
over park land to CONSTRUCTION.

## EDUCATION

Infrastructure at government schools: damaged furniture, leaking roofs,
broken fixtures, unsafe boundary walls. Hands off electrical faults serving
a school to ELECTRICITY and a structural collapse risk or major civil work
to CONSTRUCTION.

## HEALTH

Public health hazards on public land: mosquito breeding sites, unhygienic
public health facilities, biomedical waste, food safety at street vendors.
Hands off ordinary solid waste, even when called a health hazard, to
SANITATION and contaminated tap water at its source to WATER.

## FLOODING

Rainwater and waterlogging: blocked storm drains, flooded roads and
underpasses, overflowing retention ponds. Hands off foul-water overflow,
recognisable by smell and manholes rather than storm grates, to SEWAGE.

## FIRE_HAZARD

Conditions risking fire or blocking fire response: exposed wiring near
flammables, illegal storage of combustibles, blocked hydrants or fire lanes.
Hands off an electrical fault with no nearby flammable material to
ELECTRICITY and a structure blocking a fire lane, for the removal order, to
CONSTRUCTION.

## STRAY_ANIMALS

Stray and free-roaming animals on public land: aggressive dog packs, cattle
obstructing roads, suspected-rabid sightings, sterilisation requests. Hands
off a dead animal, once no longer a live hazard, to SANITATION and the
medical treatment of a bite victim to HEALTH.

## SEWAGE

The underground foul-water network: open manholes, sewer blockages and
overflow, cross-connections into stormwater lines. Hands off clean rainwater
pooling with no foul-water involvement to FLOODING and litter near a drain
mouth with no liquid overflow to SANITATION.
