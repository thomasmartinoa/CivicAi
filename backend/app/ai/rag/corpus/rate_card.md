# Municipal Rate Card

## Purpose

Work order cost estimates are grounded in the indicative unit rates below.
Rates are first-pass estimates for budgeting and approval, not final
quotes; actual contractor quotes may vary with site conditions, and the
rate card is revised annually to track material cost inflation.

## Labour and equipment rates

The standard labour rate for a general maintenance worker is ₹700 per day,
and a skilled tradesperson (electrician, plumber, mason) is billed at ₹1,100
per day. A supervising engineer's day rate is ₹2,000. The standard equipment
day rate for general-purpose machinery (compressors, generators, small
excavators) not itemised above is ₹3,500 per day; heavier plant such as a
hydraulic excavator or a road roller is billed at ₹9,000 per day.

## Notes on use

An estimate combines material line items from the table above with the
labour days a job realistically requires, plus equipment days for any
machinery involved. A work order citing this rate card should reference the
specific line items used so the estimate can be audited against the
contractor's final invoice.
